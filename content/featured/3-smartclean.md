Pattern based hybrid framework for structured data cleaning in e commerce. Benchmarked against Hugging Face FLAN T5 small and ran 63x faster at 33 milliseconds per 1000 records compared to T5's 2 seconds, with 100 percent format consistency versus T5's 90.3 percent and a 98 percent quality score. Deployed as a Flask REST API with zero API costs. Independent study research at the University of Toledo advised by Dr. Devinder Kaur.
